ABOUT ME

Curious enough
to question.
Technical enough
to build.

Online I mostly go by Max. Aryan Singh is the name on my paperwork — my COPA certificate, my trade test, the formal stuff. Both are me.

I'm interested in what happens underneath the surface — how computers work, how software is designed, how AI can be made useful, and how technology can improve the way people learn.

I completed my COPA (Computer Operator and Programming Assistant) training in 2026 and earned the National Trade Certificate. I also spend time around computer education, which keeps me close to both technology and the people learning it.

Most of what's on this page isn't a finished product — it's me teaching myself by building. Some of it works well. Some of it's rough at the edges. All of it taught me something.
